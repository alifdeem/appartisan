Artisan Side Map

Every screen an artisan sees, in the order they see it — read out of the routes, the job_status machine and the RLS policies as they actually stand today. Use it to walk the app, screenshot each state, and send them out for redesign.

16 routes 31 states worth shooting 6 of them the application funnel 120s to answer an offer
The thing this map is really for. Count the chips below: of the eleven states after a job is accepted, six are the artisan waiting on somebody else. That is the artisan product's central design problem — not the screens where they act, but the ones where they cannot. Those are the screens worth the most care in a redesign.

An artisan chooses nothing — work is offered to them. These are the four questions they have instead.

/provider

Where the redesign effort actually is. Nine of the seventeen rows are done. The six application screens are one coherent funnel and can be redesigned as a set. The two that are neither — the offer and the job — carry the most states and the most weight, and the job screen alone is eleven screenshots. Start there.
